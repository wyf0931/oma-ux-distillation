## Visible structure

The page exposed a global navigation with Products, Solutions, Industries, Shop, Drivers, and Support. The leading feature was identified as `Slide 2 of 6`, with six selectable news topics. Below it, the page exposed sections for Artificial Intelligence, Design and Simulation, High-Performance Computing, Gaming and Creating, Automotive, Robotics and Edge AI, Data Center and Cloud Computing, and About NVIDIA. Each of those sections exposed a slide count, Previous and Next buttons, and a Quick Links button.

This page map records organization only. A later DevTools MCP session clicked the main carousel's Next control and observed the panel advance from the Cybersecurity story to the Pinterest story. The tested interaction is recorded separately in `observations/nvidia/homepage-feature-carousel-next.md`.
